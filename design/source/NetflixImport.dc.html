Netflix

Netflix has no official connection, so there are two ways in.
Recommended
Import its file
On netflix.com, open Viewing activity and choose Download all. Then share the file to AiHeart. It stays on your device.
The quick download may list only titles and dates. Netflix's full data request can also include times and durations, which the late-night view needs. Check which you have.
Optional · unofficial
Sign in inside AiHeart
You sign in on Netflix's own page inside the app. AiHeart never sees your password. It reads your Viewing activity page the way you would.
Netflix may not allow automated access, and the page can change, so this can break or put your account at risk. Off by default.
Allow this
Only counts and times are used. Titles stay on your device and are never sent anywhere.
