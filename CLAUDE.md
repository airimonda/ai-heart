# AiHeart: instructions for Claude Code

## What you are building
A private iOS 26+ app with iPad, Mac and watchOS companions. Read `README.md` and `docs/` before writing code.

## Non-negotiables
1. **Private to her own devices.** Journal and body data live on her devices and sync only through her own iCloud (CloudKit private database) with encrypted attributes (`docs/11`). No other servers. No analytics, crash-reporting or ad SDKs. Other network calls are allowed only for the integrations in `docs/04-integrations.md`, and only to fetch data in. (Owner, 2026-11-02: she accepted a lyrics lookup on lrclib.net for the media vibe line. Only the title and artist of her Spotify songs (live or exported) are sent, behind a switch in Sources; lyrics are read on the phone and never stored. Nothing else is sent.)
2. **On the device by default; the cloud model is optional.** Nothing from the journal or body data leaves the device unless she turns on the cloud model (OpenRouter, her own API key in the Keychain, off by default, switch in Language model). With the switch on, the cloud model handles the text features: moment notes, Talk it through, Outlook, tone guessing, word suggestions, About me proposals, let-go and after-save checks, and Listen replies (`docs/16`, `docs/21`). Every request is stripped of personal identifiers on the phone first (`PIIRedactor`: names, places, organisations, emails, phones, links, handles, long numbers), mapped back on the phone, and sent with `data_collection: deny`. Never sent to the cloud: photos, photo and vibe captions, media (Spotify, Netflix, YouTube; rule 6), and any text that mentions self-harm. Her text is checked for self-harm on the phone first, and flagged text is handled on the phone or by fixed rules. With the switch off, everything uses the on-device model (Qwen3-VL 4B) or rules. See `docs/20`.
3. **Describe, never prescribe.** UI copy and LLM output describe patterns with uncertainty. They never give medical advice, never suggest doses, never diagnose, never label states (no "manic", "depressed episode", "disorder", and so on) unless the user wrote the word herself. (Owner, 2026-11-01: she asked to be alerted when what she logs points toward an emergency. Allowed as fixed, published red-flag rules only (blood pressure 180/120 or above, resting heart rate above 120 or below 40, chest pain, trouble breathing, fainting, confusion, and a short fixed table of combinations of logged signs). The alert describes what she logged, never names a condition, never gives a treatment, offers the local emergency number, and shows each finding once a day. No alert never means "you are fine".) (Owner, 2026-11-04: honest affirmations are allowed in notes: fixed templates in `HonestAffirmations`, chosen only when her own labelled sentences back them and filled with her words, each with the reason it is true. Never traits, promises or reassurance. Owner, 2026-11-05: honest affirmations may be written by the language model, one extra call at note time, under fixed rails checked in code: each line cites her own sentences, quotes only her exact words, and is dropped for traits, promises, dismissing reassurance, advice or orders, diagnoses, an "I" voice, or a cited sentence that mentions self-harm or reads hopeless; none are written for heavy, hopeless or self-harm entries; the fixed templates remain the fallback. See docs/16.)
4. **Gentle by design.** No streaks, no badges, no red, no guilt copy, no nagging notifications. A blank day is just blank. Empty states are calm. (Owner, 2026-10-31: she asked for a reminder to write. Allowed because she sets it: off by default, her days and time, one neutral line, skipped once she has written that day, silent while paused, planned a week ahead so it lapses if she steps away. Never add streaks or escalation to it. Owner, 2026-11-02: "When I go quiet": one non-demanding check-in after a gap longer than her usual one, once per quiet stretch, never asking her to log, off while paused, switchable in My rules. See `docs/15`.)
5. **Nothing resurfaces by itself.** No "on this day". Old entries appear only when the user opens them. (Owner, 2026-10-31: the model may read her kept entries to propose About me lines, only when she taps "Look for new things" in About me, never touching quiet topics, each line checked and added only by her.) (Owner, 2026-11-04: inside a "Talk it through" conversation she starts, the phone may search her kept entries and saved notes and send up to four short passages, redacted, with the conversation. Each is shown to her as a chip she can remove. Never released, quiet-topic or self-harm entries. See `docs/21`.)
6. **Strava and Spotify data are display-only.** Never use them for statistics, the outlook model, embeddings, or LLM prompts. See `docs/04-integrations.md` and `docs/14-automatic-sources.md`. (Owner, 2026-11-02: media from her own exports (Netflix, YouTube, Spotify "Download your data") and, at her explicit request and knowing it goes against Spotify's developer terms, songs fetched live from Spotify's API, may be read by the on-device model only to write the week's media vibe line, with lyrics looked up by title and artist (rule 1). It never feeds statistics, the outlook, embeddings or any other prompt.)
7. **Released entries are really gone** after the rest period. Deleted entries wait 30 days in Recently deleted; for a resting entry the earlier of the rest end and 30 days wins (`docs/22`). See `docs/06-privacy-and-safety.md` for what must be purged.
8. **Entries about self-harm are never released.** Keep them and open the user's Safety page.
9. **Purge wins over sync.** A released entry's tombstone beats any stale copy on any device. See `docs/11`.
11. **Listen has no persona and no memory of past sessions.** It never gives advice, never labels, and is never a crisis responder (`docs/13`). "Something to try" may suggest grounding, breathing, body exercises, reframing questions and movement, only from the fixed, evidence-labeled list in `HardMoments.catalog`, picked by the state she taps. The model never writes, picks or changes a suggestion and never writes a reframe for her (Owner, 2026-11-02). When what she said reads as heavy, a fixed card may point her to her therapist and crisis lines. Fixed words only, never the model. **"Talk it through" is separate from Listen** and has its own spec (`docs/21`): a reflective conversation she starts, in a warm voice, that may remember her own words from past talks only when she chooses "Remember it" (dated, fading with age) and may read her About me. It never diagnoses or labels, never promises, never agrees a harsh belief about herself, never gives advice she did not ask for, and uses the same fixed safety cards.
12. **No workarounds to Apple's privacy limits.** Do not try to extract Screen Time data from the Device Activity report extension. Use only supported signals.
13. **Netflix sign-in is optional and unofficial.** Off by default, in a web view with her own session, never storing credentials, and clearly labeled (`docs/14`).
10. **Design the schema for CloudKit from the first commit** (optional or defaulted attributes, no unique constraints, no deny delete rule), and decide which attributes are encrypted before the first production deploy. That cannot be changed later.

## Stack
SwiftUI multiplatform (iOS, iPadOS, native macOS, watchOS), SwiftData with CloudKit, Swift Charts, HealthKit, WidgetKit and App Intents, watchOS app, Swift Concurrency. On-device LLM: Qwen3-VL 4B (4-bit) through MLX Swift LM, behind a protocol so it can be swapped (`docs/19`). Optional cloud model: rule 2, `docs/20`. Statistics in plain Swift (Accelerate where useful). Use Dynamic Type, VoiceOver labels, and respect Reduce Motion.

Suggested layout: `App/` (iOS), `Watch/`, `Widgets/`, `Packages/AiHeartCore` (models, analytics, llm, integrations, export). Keep analytics and LLM code in the package with unit tests.

## Working rules
- Work milestone by milestone (`docs/08-roadmap.md`). Finish and test one before starting the next.
- The mockups are the source of truth for layout and copy. If the code cannot match one, say so and ask.
- Items marked **DECIDE** or listed in `docs/09-open-questions.md` need the owner's answer. Ask; do not guess.
- Keep statistics honest: show intervals, minimum-sample gates, and compare any forecast against a naive baseline.
- Test analytics with synthetic data. Never use the owner's real data in tests or fixtures.

## The private seed
`seed/about-me.private.md` is sensitive personal context. It is imported once, on first run, into the app's local encrypted "About me" store. Never commit it, print it, log it, paste it into prompts, or use it in tests. Keep `seed/` in `.gitignore`.
